-   booked: a consultation was booked or rescheduled.

-   answered: the customer got what they needed; nothing to follow up.

-   insurance_fees: Questions about session fees, sliding scale, accepted insurance, out-of-network benefits or superbills.
-   existing_client: A current client leaving a message for their clinician or the office (not a scheduling change).
-   records_forms: Requests for records, letters, release-of-information forms, intake paperwork or receipts.
-   safety_concern: Anyone mentioning suicide, self-harm, danger to themselves or others, or another crisis.
-   other: Anything that fits none of the other topics.

sentiment - positive, neutral, or negative, as the customer came across.
urgency - urgent only if the customer needs someone right away (safety, damage happening now, an emergency); low if there's no time pressure; otherwise normal.
